Draft rules

  • Three coaches this week, champion first: The Garcia Report (reigning UFC 332 King of The Ring), Doza, Rajmamba24.
  • Snake draft: 1-2-3 in odd rounds, 3-2-1 in even rounds, for 8 rounds (24 picks). All 24 fighters on the card get drafted.
  • Each coach on the clock has 90 seconds. If time expires, a random undrafted fighter is auto-picked.
  • No same-bout rule: you can draft both fighters from the same fight, and auto-pick can too — any undrafted fighter is legal.
  • New: Underdog win boost. If your drafted fighter is the BetOnline underdog (plus-money, lines locked from the Oct 9 snapshot) and wins, their total fight points are multiplied: +100 to +299 → 1.1x, +300 to +499 → 1.5x, +500 to +699 → 2x, then +0.5x for every additional +200. Losing underdogs and favorites score normally.
  • Draft from this card only. No waivers or trades this week.
  • Score Saturday night only (this event).

Draft lock: Draft locks when prelims start Sat Oct 10, 2026 at 2:00 PM PT — or when George says the draft is locked.
