The Garcia Report: Interview with Victor Escobedo

George Garcia · Apr 24, 2026

Victor Escobedo’s journey to his first amateur fight is more than just a story about stepping into the ring. It’s a story about growth, discipline, and purpose. In a recent interview, Escobedo opened up about his deep passion for Muay Thai and how the sport has become a driving force in his life.

He spoke candidly about the challenges he faced in his younger years, including navigating distractions, making mistakes, and going through phases that pulled him away from his goals. Rather than letting those experiences define him, Escobedo used them as fuel for change. Through self-reflection and determination, he has emerged more focused than ever.

Now, Escobedo represents a different version of himself, one built on discipline and commitment. Balancing two jobs while pursuing his Bachelor’s degree at California State University of Fresno, he continues to push himself both academically and physically. His dedication carries into the gym, where intense training sessions and sparring have prepared him for this moment.

As he gets ready to make his amateur debut, Escobedo stands as a testament to what can happen when passion meets perseverance. Be sure to tune in live and show your support as he steps into the ring for the first time on April 25th: 4 PM at Fresno City Community College’s Auditorium.

Quote of wisdom:

“I just wanna show my fam and my friends that I can actually commit to something and show performance that displays my knowledge and skills for something that I love and enjoy everyday. So I just think it’s not something I’ll do forever, but it’s just something that’s… In my place, in my heart, and my time in this world. I wanna show that I speak volumes for what I do. I just wanna show actions for what I talk about, and for what I do on the daily.”

–Victor Escobedo
