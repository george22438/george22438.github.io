UFC Vegas 121: Rosas vs Barcelos — BetOnline Moneylines

George Garcia · Sep 22, 2026

Saturday night at the APEX does not ask for poetry first. It asks for a number.

BetOnline has posted American moneylines for every bout on UFC Fight Night: Rosas Jr. vs. Barcelos — also billed as UFC Vegas 121 — and this board is the story before the cage lights up. Main card goes at 5:00 PM PT on Paramount+. Prelims open at 2:00 PM PT. Lines checked September 22, 2026 on BetOnline.ag. Odds move. Fighters do too.

Lines are not destiny. They are the room’s current opinion — loud, temporary, and worth reading with both eyes open.

Main Card — 5:00 PM PT

Raul Rosas Jr. (−176) vs Raoni Barcelos (+149)

Bantamweight. Five rounds. The kid is the favorite for a reason — pace, pressure, that early-career hunger that still looks unfinished. Barcelos is the plus-money veteran answer: experience, timing, the kind of counter that makes a favorite honest. BetOnline makes Rosas Jr. a solid chalk, not a lock.

Rodolfo Vieira (−152) vs Robert Bryczek (+130)

Middleweight. Mild favorite for Vieira. Grappling gravity versus a man who came to spoil plans. Close enough that a single round of chaos can rewrite the ticket.

Mehemmedeli Osmanli (−279) vs Ilimbek Akylbek Uulu (+231)

Bantamweight. The Ultimate Fighter 34 men’s final. Heavy favorite for Osmanli. The board is saying one man’s ceiling looks higher tonight — plus money still buys a dream if the underdog’s hands arrive first.

Valesca Machado (−220) vs Melissa Amaya (+185)

Women’s strawweight. TUF 34 women’s final. Machado (also known as Tina Black) is clear favorite at −220. Amaya is the live plus price if the fight turns into a scramble of will.

Rinya Nakamura (−335) vs Brady Hiestand (+273)

Bantamweight. Heavy favorite for Nakamura. Hiestand’s plus money is the “if everything breaks” ticket — the kind of number that only pays when the favorite’s night goes sideways.

Prelims — 2:00 PM PT

Rodolfo Bellato (−175) vs Christian Edwards (+149)

Light heavyweight. Solid favorite for Bellato. Edwards sits in the classic underdog seat: survive the early storm, steal late minutes.

Josiah Harrell (−131) vs Elves Brener (+112)

Lightweight. The closest line on the card — a true toss-up. BetOnline leans Harrell by a whisper. This is where card-players lean in.

Montel Jackson (−205) vs Ricky Simon (+173)

Bantamweight. Clear favorite for Jackson. Simon’s plus money remembers every time a ranked vet made favorite money sweat.

Ailin Perez (−134) vs Norma Dumont (+115)

Women’s bantamweight. Near toss-up. Ranked pride on both sides. Perez gets a slight nod; Dumont is barely an underdog — the kind of number that means the room cannot decide.

John Castaneda (−330) vs Alatengheili (+269)

Bantamweight. Heavy favorite for Castaneda. Alatengheili (Heili Alateng) is deep plus money — knockout lottery territory if the favorite blinks.

Mickey Gall (−155) vs Sedriques Dumas (+132)

Middleweight. Mild favorite for Gall. Dumas at plus money is the spoiler price.

Yazmin Jauregui (−980) vs Vanessa Demopoulos (+663)

Women’s strawweight. The biggest favorite on the board. Jauregui is massive chalk. Demopoulos is the longest shot of the night — the number you take only if you believe in miracles and mid-fight chaos.
