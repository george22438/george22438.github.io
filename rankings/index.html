Zuffa Boxing

As of Sep. 24, 2026 · Source: UFC.com — Zuffa Boxing + linked reports below

Zuffa Boxing hasn't published its own rankings yet

Zuffa Boxing has not published official rankings, top 15 or otherwise. It recognizes eight divisions with one champion each. Below: the eight divisions and the one champion ufc.com confirms. We don't make up rankings.

Reports say Zuffa Boxing uses The Ring magazine's ratings to set up matchups and pick title challengers while it builds its own system (BoxingScene, Feb. 2026). The Ring's ratings are independent and cover all of boxing, not just Zuffa fighters, so we don't show them here as Zuffa rankings.
