About The Garcia Report

Passionate, honest coverage with heart and grit — boxing and MMA journalism from Fresno and beyond.

George Garcia

George Garcia is the voice behind The Garcia Report: a fight writer covering boxing and MMA with the same intensity the athletes bring into the cage and the ring.

Based in Fresno, California, George writes about legends returning, rising killers, local fighters chasing their first belts, and the chaos that still makes combat sports feel alive.

No fluff. No manufactured narratives. Just honest takes — picks, interviews, live notes, and stories that respect the grit it takes to fight.
